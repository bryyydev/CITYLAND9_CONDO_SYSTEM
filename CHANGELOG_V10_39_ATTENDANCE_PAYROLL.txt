CITYLAND 9 CONDO SYSTEM V10.39
Employee Attendance & Payroll

Added:
- Daily employee attendance recording
- Time-in and time-out
- Present, Absent, Late, Undertime, Leave and Rest Day statuses
- Attendance remarks
- Attendance date filtering
- Payroll period selection
- Basic salary, overtime, allowances and deductions
- Automatic absence and late/undertime deductions from attendance
- Net pay calculation
- Payroll Draft/Final/Paid status
- Printable payroll slip
- New Attendance and Payroll navigation links

Note:
Payroll uses the employee monthly salary already stored in the Employee module when Basic Salary is left blank. The initial absence calculation uses a 26-working-day divisor. This is an initial payroll framework and should be configured to the condominium's approved payroll rules before actual salary processing.

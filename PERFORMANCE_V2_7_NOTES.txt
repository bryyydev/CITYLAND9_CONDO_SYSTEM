FINAL 5.1 Performance V2.7 - Billing/SOA and Water performance fixes

Changes:
- Billing/SOA GET is now read-only: advance allocation and penalty refresh are no longer executed on every page load.
- Billing/SOA water synchronization now uses one monthly WaterReading query instead of one query per bill.
- Billing calculation cache loads only the selected month's water readings when the Billing page is being rendered. Historical bill calculations use the stored water charge.
- Billing unit relationships now eager-load current tenants to avoid N+1 queries in the Billing table.
- Water Readings now eager-loads Unit relationships to avoid N+1 unit queries.
- Water Readings no longer loads every Billing ORM record just to determine water payment status; payment status is derived from WaterReading data.
- Existing Advance Condo Dues, overdue penalty, Billing/SOA and LAN startup features are preserved.

Important:
- Keep your existing cityland_condo_web.db.
- Run START_WINDOWS.bat only.

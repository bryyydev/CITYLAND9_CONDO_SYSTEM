V10.23 – WATER PAID STATUS SYNC

- Fixed the SOA Water Reading History so clicking Paid on a water reading immediately makes that reading Paid in the SOA.
- The reading's own Paid flag now takes priority over the overall monthly SOA billing status.
- Paid water readings are therefore hidden from the SOA Water Reading History as requested.
- Unpaid, Partially Paid, and Overdue readings continue to use the corresponding monthly billing status.
- Updated the Water Readings table to use the same status helper for consistent display.
- Marking a water reading Paid does NOT mark the entire condo SOA as paid.

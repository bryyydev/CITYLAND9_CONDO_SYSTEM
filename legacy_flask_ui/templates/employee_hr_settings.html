Payroll Rules & Philippine Statutory Contributions

Configure the payroll rules used by Employee Payroll. Changes apply to newly generated payroll records.

Attendance & Salary Rules

The default daily salary divisor is 26 days. Late/undertime is calculated from recorded time-in/time-out when the attendance status supports it. The standard schedule assumed by the automatic calculation is 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM; the status can still be used for manual control.

SSS

Default values follow the SSS schedule used by this build: 15% total SS contribution, shared 10% employer / 5% employee, plus employer-paid EC. SSS states that the applicable contribution is based on its latest schedule.

PhilHealth

The default is 5% with equal employee/employer sharing and a ₱10,000–₱100,000 contribution base. Verify against the latest PhilHealth table before final payroll approval.

Pag-IBIG

The contribution settings are editable so payroll staff can update the applicable Pag-IBIG schedule without changing program code.

BIR Withholding Tax — Monthly

The payroll calculation treats employee SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG shares as mandatory deductions before the monthly taxable base. BIR's official calculator identifies these employee shares as deductions from compensation for withholding-tax computation.

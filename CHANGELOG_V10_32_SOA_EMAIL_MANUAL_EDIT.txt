V10.32 – SOA Email Center and Manual SOA Corrections

- Generate Bills now opens the SOA Email Center.
- Added per-unit email selection and Send All Opted-In.
- Owner and current tenants can independently opt in to receive SOA by email.
- Added safe schema migrations for receive_soa_email and soa_note.
- Added owner editing with SOA email preference.
- Added Super Admin/Admin manual SOA correction for charges, balances, penalty, adjustment, due date and correction note.
- Manual corrections are recorded in Audit Logs.
- Added admin role to user creation and Billing/Email access.
- Existing data is preserved.
- Added a manual-override flag so corrected SOA charge values are retained instead of being recalculated on the next view.

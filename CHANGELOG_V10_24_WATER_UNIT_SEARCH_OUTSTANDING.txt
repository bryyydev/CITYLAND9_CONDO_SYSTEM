CityLand 9 Condo Web System - V10.24

Water Reading Unit Search - Outstanding Months

Changes:
- On the Water Readings tab, searching by Unit Number now shows a separate Outstanding Water Readings section below the normal search results.
- The outstanding section includes historical water-reading months that are Unpaid, Partially Paid, or Overdue.
- The outstanding-month search is based on the searched Unit Number and intentionally ignores the selected Billing Month filter so older unpaid/overdue months are not missed.
- Paid water readings remain excluded from the outstanding section.
- Existing Water Reading search/filter behavior remains unchanged.

Tested:
- app.py passes Python syntax compilation with python -m py_compile.

V10.3 FEATURE UPDATE

Implemented requested condominium management features:
- Statement of Account (SOA) can be viewed and printed from Billing.
- SOA includes owner/tenant details, unit overdue months, parking details, parking overdue months, charges, balances, and payment history.
- Unit creation now has an Owner/Tenant occupancy dropdown.
- Multiple tenants are retained in the Tenants database with history/status for future references.
- Email fields are optional everywhere; blank email is accepted.
- “With Parking” reveals parking assignment controls on Add Unit.
- Parking supports Owner/Tenant assignment and per-square-meter fees.
- Added dedicated Parking tab/page with unit, owner/tenant, slot, sqm, rate, monthly fee, and overdue-month tracking.
- Monthly billing creates per-parking billing records for future parking-specific tracking.
- Water page now shows historical readings and payment status filters: Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid.
- Existing database is preserved; schema migration adds the new occupancy and parking-billing structures.

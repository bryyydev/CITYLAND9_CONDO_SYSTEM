CITYLAND 9 CONDO SYSTEM 2026 - V10.38 REPORTS

Added a full Reports module based on the existing V10.37 system.

Features:
- Daily report preset
- Weekly report preset (Monday-Sunday)
- Monthly report preset
- Yearly report preset
- Custom date range
- Bills generated count and total billed
- Billing collections
- Direct water collections
- Advance payments received
- Current outstanding balance
- Expenses and net cash flow
- Collections by CASH / CHECK / ONLINE
- Billing assessment summary
- Detailed collection transaction list
- Detailed expense list
- Print-friendly report
- Excel export for selected date range

Reports use transaction dates for collections/expenses and bill creation dates for bills generated.
Existing V10.37 billing, unpaid and advance functions are preserved.

V10.26 - Detailed SOA Charges, Water-Paid Exclusion, and Penalty Recalculation

Changes:
- SOA DESCRIPTION now shows billing month/year for condo dues, e.g. SEPTEMBER 2026 DUES.
- SOA water description now shows month, consumption, and rate, e.g. SEPTEMBER 2026 WATER — 12.00 m³ × ₱50.00/m³.
- A water reading explicitly marked Paid is omitted from the SOA charges and from the displayed SOA total.
- SOA penalty is recalculated using the latest prior outstanding monthly balance (including remaining water and other charges), plus the current bill balance only when the current bill is overdue.
- Penalty description shows the computation: Total Month Balance × configured penalty rate (default 10%).
- SOA total is recalculated using the effective charges after excluding paid water and applying the recalculated penalty.
- Existing Water Reading paid/edit behavior is retained.

CityLand 9 Condo Web System V10.17

New features:
1. Rates & Rules
   - Added Automatic Water Computation setting (enabled by default).
   - Water charge is automatically calculated from cubic-meter consumption x configurable Water Rate / m³.
   - Water rate remains user-editable globally and per individual water reading.

2. Billing / SOA
   - Added Send Email action beside View SOA and Print.
   - Email is sent to the current tenant representative when available, otherwise the current owner/unit email.
   - Added SMTP Host, Port, Sender Email, Username and Password fields under Rates & Rules.
   - Email sends an HTML summary of the SOA.

3. Water Readings
   - Added Paid action beside Edit.
   - Paid status is stored independently on each water reading with paid date.
   - Water history filter now uses the water reading's own Paid/Unpaid status.

4. Database
   - Added safe schema upgrade for water_reading.paid and water_reading.paid_date for existing SQLite, SQL Server and PostgreSQL databases.
   - Excel database export/import now includes water paid fields.
   - Excel water reading dates are parsed into Python date objects.

5. Print SOA
   - Water Reading History remains without Status in print output.
   - Print-only controls remain hidden.

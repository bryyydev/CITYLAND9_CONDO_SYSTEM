CITYLAND 9 CONDO WEB SYSTEM V10.42 - PENALTY SELECTION FIXED

Penalty Rule now has four independent checkboxes:
- Condo Dues
- Parking
- Storage
- Water

Default: Condo Dues selected; Parking, Storage and Water unselected.

The selected components are used consistently by Billing and SOA. Penalty is calculated
from outstanding selected charge types from prior billing months and only when the
current bill is overdue. Payments are allocated Condo Dues -> Parking -> Storage -> Water
for determining the remaining penalty base. Advance payments continue to apply to Condo
Dues only.

Also fixed the Rates & Rules POST handler so unchecked boxes are saved as 0 instead of
retaining the previous setting.

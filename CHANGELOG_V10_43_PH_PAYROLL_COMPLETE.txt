CITYLAND 9 CONDO SYSTEM 2026
V10.43 - Philippine Payroll & HR Enhancement

Based on V10.42 PENALTY SELECTION FIXED2.

Added:
1. Employee Attendance remains included.
2. Payroll remains included.
3. Automatic approved overtime inclusion when OT Pay is left blank.
4. Automatic recurring loan/deduction inclusion from active HR loans.
5. Automatic attendance absence deduction using configurable working-day divisor.
6. Automatic late/undertime deduction using recorded time-in/time-out, configurable grace period,
   and the standard 8:00 AM / 5:00 PM schedule used by this build.
7. Configurable Philippine statutory payroll rules page: Employees / HR -> Payroll Rules.
8. SSS employee/employer rates, maximum MSC, EC thresholds and EC amounts are configurable.
9. PhilHealth rate, contribution floor/ceiling and employee share are configurable.
10. Pag-IBIG contribution base and employee/employer rates are configurable.
11. BIR monthly withholding-tax thresholds and rates are configurable.
12. 13th-month tax-free ceiling setting is included for payroll policy configuration.
13. Payroll printout now shows SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR withholding tax and employer statutory cost.
14. Government statutory breakdown remains available per payroll record.
15. Existing V10.42 penalty-selection feature is retained.
16. Existing startup-order fix is retained.

Important payroll note:
The system provides a configurable payroll computation framework. Government schedules can change.
Payroll/accounting staff must verify the applicable official SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR
rules before finalizing payroll and remittances.

Database:
No existing business tables are intentionally deleted. New HR settings are stored in the existing
EmployeeHRSetting table and are created automatically if missing.
